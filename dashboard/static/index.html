Small/mid-caps in the market's hottest bottleneck industries, split into two evidence-backed sleeves: Momentum (strong & still trending) and Reversal (beaten down, turning back up). Rising volume + real fundamentals (quality & growth) favored; distress, dilution, negative-margin junk and true blow-offs are screened out. Scoring is regime-aware: when breadth is weak the engine trusts momentum less and leans on fundamentals; when breadth is healthy it gives reversals more room.
