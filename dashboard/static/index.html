Chart Patterns

Classical setups detected across weekly, daily, and 4-hour bars: inverse head & shoulders (including the double-bottom-with-a-higher-low variant), H&S tops, double bottoms/tops, flags, triangles, wedges, cup & handle, and long-term range breakouts/breakdowns. Forming = price has never crossed the trigger line — the early entry window; Retest = it broke through and pulled back to the line without the measured move playing out — the throwback, the second-chance entry; Breakout = crossed within the last few bars and not yet extended. Stale, extended, or played-out breaks are dropped automatically, so everything here is live. Weekly/daily read closing-price structure; 4H uses full highs/lows. An intraday alert fires the moment a forming or retesting name crosses its trigger. Refreshed daily at 6:45 AM ET (4H again at 12:45 PM). Click any row for the full chart drawer.

Watchtower Oscillator

Momentum + money-flow composite on confirmed bars only — the live bar never enters the math, so a signal that's here at the close can never repaint away. The wave pair (WaveTrend) marks stretch: beyond ±53 is an actionable extreme, beyond ±60 is blown out. Money flow marks fuel, Williams %R(28) marks pinning, MACD confirms. Entry grade (the default) demands quality on top of the oscillator: a live pattern in the trade direction within reach of its trigger, a weekly that isn't fighting the trade, and real relative strength — the setups you'd actually enter. Loaded spring is the backtest's best cohort (+4.4%/+2.7% vs SPY per 21d): money flow or %R dips while RSI refuses to give up 50 — shallow digestion in a strong name, always bullish. Washout watch is the raw stretched-and-turning list: the most oversold names in the market, where turns start — stalk them until they build the higher low, don't buy the first green dot. Divergence stack counts (2/4, 3/4…) are context, not conviction — the replay shows stacking doesn't add edge. Bearish setups here are warnings on longs, not short entries (every bearish oscillator cohort has positive forward returns); shorts come from the Patterns tab. Refreshed with the pattern scans: full at 6:45 AM ET, 4H/1H again at 12:45 PM. Click a row for the chart drawer.
